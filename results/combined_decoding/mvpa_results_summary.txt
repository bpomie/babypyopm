MVPA RESULTS SUMMARY
====================

Analysis: Temporal decoding of frequent vs infrequent tones
Method: Sliding estimator with logistic regression
Cross-validation: 5-fold stratified
Scoring metric: Area Under the ROC Curve (AUC)

RESULTS
-------
Peak decoding time: 230 ms
Peak group AUC: 0.737
Mean AUC at peak (± SD): 0.737 ± 0.000

Individual subject AUCs at peak:
  S104: 0.737

STATISTICAL NOTE
----------------
Given the small sample size (n=1), formal statistical inference
is limited. However, a binomial test at group peak decoding time showed
that 1/1 participants decoded above chance level
(AUC > 0.5), p = 0.500.
